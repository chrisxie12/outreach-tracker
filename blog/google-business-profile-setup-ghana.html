How to Set Up Your Google Business Profile in Ghana

Aug 20264 min read

If your business is not on Google Business Profile, you are missing out on one of the most powerful free tools available. When someone in your area searches for your type of business on Google or Google Maps, your profile is what shows up. It is completely free and can dramatically increase your visibility.

Setting up your Google Business Profile is straightforward. Start by going to business.google.com and signing in with your Google account. Search for your business name to see if a listing already exists. If it does, you can claim it. If not, click "Add your business" and follow the prompts to enter your business name, category, and location.

Google will need to verify your business, usually through a postcard sent to your address, a phone call, or email verification. This step is important because it proves to Google that your business is real and located where you say it is. Once verified, your profile will start appearing in search results and on Google Maps.

After verification, complete every section of your profile. Add your business hours, phone number, website, and a detailed description of what you do. Upload high-quality photos of your business, products, or services. Profiles with photos get significantly more clicks and direction requests than those without.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Inconsistent information: Make sure your business name, address, and phone number are exactly the same everywhere online.
  • Ignoring reviews: Respond to every review, positive or negative. It shows you care about customer feedback.
  • Outdated hours: Update your hours for holidays and special events to avoid frustrating customers.
  • No photos: A profile without photos looks incomplete and untrustworthy.

To optimize your profile, post regular updates, add new photos weekly, and use the Q&A section to answer common questions.
