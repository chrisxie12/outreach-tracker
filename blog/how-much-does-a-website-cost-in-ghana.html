How Much Does a Website Cost in Ghana in 2026?

Aug 20265 min read

If you are a small business owner in Ghana wondering how much a website costs, you are not alone. Many business owners hear wildly different prices and are unsure what to expect. The truth is that website costs in Ghana depend on what you need, but there is a clear range for every type of business.

At the simplest level, a landing page is a single-page website designed to do one thing: convert visitors into customers. Whether you are promoting a service, running an ad campaign, or collecting leads, a landing page is the fastest and most affordable way to get online. Most landing pages in Ghana cost between GHc1500 and GHc3500 depending on the design and features.

A full business website with multiple pages (home, about, services, contact, etc.) gives your business a professional home on the internet. This is what most customers expect when they search for your business on Google. A well-designed business website in Ghana typically costs between GHc3500 and GHc8000, depending on the number of pages, design complexity, and any special features you need.

If you want to sell products online, an e-commerce website is what you need. This includes product listings, a shopping cart, payment integration, and order management. E-commerce websites start at around GHc10000 and can go higher depending on the number of products and payment gateways you want to integrate.

What affects the price of a website?

  • Number of pages: More pages mean more design and content work, which increases the cost.
  • Design complexity: A custom design with animations and interactive elements costs more than a clean, simple layout.
  • Features and functionality: Contact forms, booking systems, payment integration, and member areas all add to the cost.
  • Content creation: If you need copywriting, photography, or product descriptions, this may be included or charged separately.
  • Maintenance and hosting: Some designers include ongoing support, while others charge separately for hosting and updates.

Choosing the right option depends on your goals and budget. If you are just starting out, a landing page or simple business website is a smart first step.
