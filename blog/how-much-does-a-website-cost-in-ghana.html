How Much Does a Website Cost in Ghana in 2026?

Aug 20267 min read

If you are a small business owner in Ghana wondering how much a website costs, you are not alone. Many business owners hear wildly different prices — a friend says GH₵500, a freelancer quotes GH₵15,000, and an agency says "it depends." The truth is that website costs in Ghana follow a clear pattern, and understanding it helps you avoid overpaying or settling for something that does not work.

Whether you run a chop bar in Osu, a hair salon in Tema, or a retail shop in Kumasi, your website is often the first impression a customer gets. Google data shows that most Ghanaians search on their phones before visiting a business. If your website is slow, outdated, or non-existent, those customers go elsewhere.

Landing pages: GH₵1,500 – GH₵3,500

A landing page is a single-page website designed to do one thing: convert visitors into customers. It works best when you are running a specific promotion, running Facebook or Instagram ads, or collecting leads for a service.

For example, a photography studio in Accra running a "Family Portrait December Special" campaign can send ad traffic to a landing page with one clear offer, a gallery, and a WhatsApp button. No navigation, no extra pages — just the offer and a way to respond. This is the fastest and most affordable way to get online.

What you typically get: mobile-responsive design, contact form or WhatsApp integration, your business name and branding, and hosting for one year.

Business websites: GH₵3,500 – GH₵8,000

A full business website with multiple pages (home, about, services, contact, etc.) gives your business a professional home on the internet. This is what most customers expect when they search for your business on Google.

Consider a local bakery in Accra that wants to showcase its wedding cakes, event catering, and daily menu. A 5-6 page website with a gallery, pricing page, and WhatsApp ordering button gives customers everything they need to make a decision — without calling first.

A well-designed business website in Ghana typically costs between GH₵3,500 and GH₵8,000, depending on the number of pages, design complexity, and any special features you need. Most projects in this range take 10-21 days to complete.

E-commerce websites: GH₵10,000+

If you want to sell products online — clothing, accessories, electronics, or food — an e-commerce website is what you need. This includes product listings, a shopping cart, payment integration (Mobile Money, cards, bank transfer), and order management.

A fashion boutique in Accra selling handmade accessories can list 50-200 products, accept Mobile Money payments through providers like Hubtel or Paystack, and manage orders from a dashboard. E-commerce sites start at around GH₵10,000 and can go higher depending on the number of products and payment gateways you want to integrate.

What affects the price?

  • Number of pages: More pages mean more design and content work. A 3-page site costs less than a 10-page site.
  • Design complexity: A custom design with animations costs more than a clean, simple layout using a proven template.
  • Features: Contact forms, booking systems, payment integration, and member areas all add to the cost.
  • Content creation: If you need professional copywriting, product photography, or logo design, this may be included or charged separately.
  • Maintenance and hosting: Some designers include ongoing support, others charge separately. Hosting in Ghana typically costs GH₵200-800 per year.

How to choose the right option

Before you request a quote, answer these three questions:

  1. What is the goal? If you just need people to call you, a landing page is enough. If you want to showcase multiple services, you need a business website. If you want to sell products, you need e-commerce.
  2. Who is your customer? If they search on Google, you need a website optimized for mobile. If they find you on Instagram, a landing page with a strong CTA might be all you need.
  3. What is your budget? Start with what you can afford now. A landing page today is better than waiting six months for a "perfect" website.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website in Ghana?

A landing page takes 3-5 days. A business website takes 10-21 days. An e-commerce site takes 30-45 days. Timelines depend on how quickly you provide content (text, images, logo) and feedback.

Do I need to pay for hosting and domain separately?

Yes. Domain names cost around GH₵50-150 per year (.com or .com.gh). Hosting ranges from GH₵200-800 per year depending on your provider. Some agencies bundle these into the project price — always ask what is included.

Can I get a website for free?

Free website builders like Wix or Google Sites exist, but they come with limitations: no custom domain, ads on your site, limited design control, and poor Google rankings. For a serious business, a professional website is a small investment that pays for itself.

Should I use a Ghanaian developer or an international one?

A local developer understands the Ghanaian market — Mobile Money integration, local hosting, WhatsApp as a primary contact channel, and the specific challenges businesses face here. International developers may not understand these nuances.

Choosing the right option depends on your goals and budget. If you are just starting out, a landing page or simple business website is a smart first step. If you are ready to sell online, investing in an e-commerce site will pay off in the long run. Either way, the first step is understanding what you need — and that starts with a free audit.
